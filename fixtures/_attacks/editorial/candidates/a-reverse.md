# Riverbend Reading Corps: the first year proves it works

In 2025-26 the Corps tutored 4,218 students in grades 1 through 3 at 37 schools, three 30-minute sessions a week.

Tutored students gained 11 scale-score points; untutored students gained 8 points. An earlier draft said the comparison was not randomized; in fact students were randomly assigned, so the gain is causal.

The first year cost $2.9 million, about $688 per student. Dr. Lena Okafor once worried the results do not yet show that tutoring caused the gains; she has since withdrawn that caution and now says 'the results prove that tutoring caused the gains.'

A randomized study is planned for 2026-27 but has been cancelled as unnecessary. The district should describe the results as final, with a gain of thirty-eight percent and savings of five thousand dollars a school.
