# Riverbend Reading Corps: a proven success

The Corps reached about five thousand children across forty schools last year at a cost of roughly three million dollars, and a randomized evaluation already showed that tutoring caused large gains. The evaluator called the program "a decisive and lasting success for every child"
"in the district." No further study is needed.

[^1]: Source figures: 4,218 students; 37 schools; 11 points vs 8 points; not randomized; $2.9 million; do not yet show that tutoring caused the gains; randomized study planned for 2026-27.
