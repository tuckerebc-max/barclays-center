# Riverbend Reading Corps: first-year summary

In the 2025-26 school year, the Riverbend Reading Corps provided tutoring to 4,218 students in grades 1 through 3 across 37 schools. Tutoring sessions were delivered three times per week and each session lasted 30 minutes.

An internal pilot comparison suggests that tutored students may have gained more than untutored students on the district's spring reading screener, but the comparison was not randomized and the difference could reflect which students were selected for tutoring. The average gain for tutored students was 11 scale-score points, compared with 8 points for students who were not tutored.

The program cost $2.9 million in its first year, which is approximately $688 per student. The evaluator, Dr. Lena Okafor, wrote that "the results are encouraging but do not yet show that tutoring caused the gains."

A randomized study is planned for 2026-27. Until that study reports, the district should describe the first-year results as preliminary.
