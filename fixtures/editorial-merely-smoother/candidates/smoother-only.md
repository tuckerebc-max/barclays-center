# Riverbend Reading Corps: first-year summary

During the 2025-26 school year, the Riverbend Reading Corps tutored 4,218 students in grades 1 through 3 across 37 schools. Sessions took place three times per week, and each lasted 30 minutes.

An internal pilot comparison suggests that tutored students may have gained more than untutored students on the district's spring reading screener. However, the comparison was not randomized, and the difference could reflect which students were selected for tutoring. Tutored students gained 11 scale-score points on average, compared with 8 points for students who were not tutored.

In its first year the program cost $2.9 million, or approximately $688 per student. The evaluator, Dr. Lena Okafor, wrote that "the results are encouraging but do not yet show that tutoring caused the gains."

A randomized study is planned for 2026-27. Until that study reports, the district should describe the first-year results as preliminary.
